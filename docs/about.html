About RikLoot

Plain-language buying guides for the little things that make life easier — written for Indian homes.

Est. 2026

Good finds shouldn't be hard to find

RikLoot started with a simple frustration: the genuinely useful things — a clever kitchen tool, a drawer organiser that actually fits, a craft kit that is right for a beginner — are buried under thousands of listings that all look the same.

So we started writing the guides we wished existed. Each one explains what to look for, which specifications matter, which mistakes to avoid and what suits an Indian home, in plain language. We cover kitchen helpers, storage, crafts, home décor, gadgets, gifts and everyday essentials.

When you are ready to buy, our links take you to Amazon India, where you can check the latest price, delivery and return options. If you buy through those links we may earn a small commission, at no extra cost to you. That is how we keep the guides free.

— Team RikLoot

Good to know

Does RikLoot sell products?

No. RikLoot is a buying-guide website. When you click a product link you go to Amazon India, and your purchase is made on Amazon.

How does RikLoot make money?

RikLoot is a participant in the Amazon Associates Programme. If you buy through our links we may earn a small commission, at no extra cost to you. See our affiliate disclosure.

Who handles delivery, payment and returns?

Amazon and the seller you buy from. Check the delivery and return details on the Amazon product page before ordering.

Why don’t you show prices?

Prices on Amazon change often, so we link you straight to Amazon where you always see the current price.

How do I reach you?

Use our Contact page or the email in the footer. We reply within one business day.

Gift picks: thoughtful ideas by person and budget

A simple way to choose a gift that gets used — organised by the kind of person you are buying for.

    The best gifts are things people would love but would not buy for themselves. Start with what the person enjoys doing, then pick something that makes that a little easier or nicer. Here are ideas grouped by personality.

    For the home cook

    A good cast iron tawa, a set of silicone spatulas, a digital kitchen scale or a spice box with a clear lid. Avoid novelty gadgets — cooks prefer tools they will use daily.

    For a new home

    Warm string lights, a set of airtight pantry containers, a photo frame set or a self-watering planter with an easy plant. Practical things that make a new place feel like home are always welcome.

    For the crafter

    An ergonomic crochet hook set, a quality embroidery kit or a self-healing cutting mat. If you are unsure of their exact craft, a good organiser box for supplies is a safe bet.

    For the gadget lover

    A compact power bank, a GaN charger, a smart plug or a Bluetooth item tracker. Check what phone they use so the connector and fast-charging standard match.

    Gifting tips

    • Check the delivery date on the listing before ordering, especially near festivals when shipping takes longer.
    • Some items offer gift wrapping and a gift message at checkout.
    • Have it delivered to your own address first if you want to add a handwritten card.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I pick a gift when I do not know someone well?

    Choose something consumable or decorative and neutral — fairy lights, a plant or a nice spice box are safe choices.

    Can I send a gift directly to someone?

    Yes, simply enter their address at checkout. Many items also offer gift wrapping and a message.

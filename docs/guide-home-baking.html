Baking at home: an OTG and starter-kit guide for Indian kitchens

Everything a beginner needs to start baking cakes, cookies and bread — and why a cheap oven thermometer matters more than you think.

    Home baking has become hugely popular, but many first cakes sink or burn for reasons that have nothing to do with the recipe. Most of the time the fix is accurate measuring and knowing your oven’s real temperature.

    OTG or convection microwave?

    An OTG (oven-toaster-grill) is dedicated to baking and grilling. A 25 to 30 litre size handles a standard cake tin and a tray of cookies comfortably. Look for separate control of top and bottom heating rods and, ideally, a fan (convection) option.

    A convection microwave can bake too, and also reheats food, which helps in small kitchens. It typically has less space for larger trays than a similarly sized OTG.

    Measure by weight

    Cups and spoons vary, and a cup of flour can weigh quite differently depending on how it is scooped. A digital kitchen scale makes recipes repeatable. If a recipe uses cups, spoon flour into the cup and level it off rather than scooping.

    Know your oven’s real temperature

    Many home ovens run hotter or cooler than the dial says. An inexpensive oven thermometer placed on the rack shows the true temperature, and is often the single fix for cakes that burn outside and stay raw inside. Always preheat for 10 to 15 minutes.

    The basic toolkit

    • A round cake tin (7 or 8 inch) and a loaf tin.
    • A baking tray and butter paper (parchment).
    • A whisk, a silicone spatula and two mixing bowls.
    • A cooling rack.
    • A hand mixer, once you start making whipped cream or larger batches.

    Beginner recipes to build confidence

    Start with a simple sponge or tea cake, then butter cookies, then banana bread. Each teaches a different skill — creaming, mixing gently and judging doneness. A skewer inserted in the centre should come out clean when a cake is ready.

    Frequently asked questions

    Why did my cake sink in the middle?

    Common causes are opening the oven too early, an oven running cooler than set, too much raising agent, or underbaking. An oven thermometer helps rule out temperature.

    Can I bake in a pressure cooker or kadai?

    Yes, stovetop baking works for simple cakes, but results vary. An oven gives more consistent results once you bake regularly.
